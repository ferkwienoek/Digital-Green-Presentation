Digital Green · FarmerChat

A farmer gets advice.
Did it help?

It's a simple question, and a hard one to answer for millions of people. Field Echo asks it gently, one farmer at a time, about the advice they were actually given.

The problem

Giving advice is the easy part.
Knowing if it helped is not.

FarmerChat answers millions of farmers in their own language. But understanding whether that advice was followed, and whether it worked, still depends on field studies that take months and only ever reach a small sample.

0
farmers rely on FarmerChat

Studies take months

A single field study runs for months and reaches only a few thousand people.

One survey for all

Everyone gets the same questions, no matter what they actually asked about.

So most farmers are never asked, and the ones who are rarely get a question that fits.

Our idea

A follow-up that fits
the advice each farmer received.

  1. 01

    FarmerChat remembers each conversation, the question the farmer asked, the advice they were given, and how it was resolved.

  2. 02

    From that, the agent writes a short survey about their advice, in their language, at a point in the season when the answer will mean something.

  3. 03

    Each survey is checked before it's sent. If a conversation has nothing worth following up on, the farmer simply isn't contacted.

A good question at the right moment, and nothing more.

Why it matters

A clearer picture
of what's working in the field.

Rather than drawing conclusions from a small sample, Digital Green can hear back from farmers across the whole season. The rigorous field trials still matter. This fills in the long stretches between them.

Every conversation counts

Each resolved chat becomes a real signal, not a once-a-study sample.

In the farmer's language

Hindi today, with more languages and a voice version on the way.

Respectful of their time

Short, relevant, and never anything off-topic or intrusive.

What it takes to adopt

Light to run,
and built to be handed over.

  • It fits into what Digital Green already runs, so there's nothing new for them to learn or maintain.
  • Most conversations are handled without calling a model at all, which keeps the cost small even as it grows.
  • It's open source, so other organizations working with farmers can use it too.

Starting with a pilot of 10,000 farmers, with room to grow from there.
